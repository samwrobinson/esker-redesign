What "Custom" Means Here, and What It Usually Means

A lot of Vermont web design is sold as custom. Ask these four questions and you will know which kind you are getting:

  • Is it a theme? If the answer involves WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, or Divi, the layout was designed for someone else first.
  • Will I see the design before it is built? If the first thing you see is the live site, the design step was skipped.
  • What does it score on Google PageSpeed? Ours score 95 to 100. Most theme sites score 40 to 70 on a phone.
  • What do I own if I leave? With us, everything. The design files, the code, the domain, the content.
