We Plan Every Migration to Protect Your Rankings

Before beginning a website migration, we catalogue every single URL, page, and piece of content on your website. We gain an understanding of what is ranking well, what needs improvements, and create an action plan for the migration.

We preserve your high performing content, improve your underperforming content, and make sure that we set up the proper redirects to preserve traffic to any pages that we have to change during the migration process.

Zero Downtime for Your Existing Website

We build and test your new site on a private staging environment first. This means your existing website keeps running while we work, and keeps making you money, right up until the moment we go live.

Before anything goes public, we test every redirect, every form, every checkout and booking flow, on both desktop and mobile. After launch, we run a full sweep for broken links and 404 errors and confirm your analytics, tracking, and email are all working exactly as they should.

When it's time to launch, we schedule the switch for your lowest-traffic hours and handle the DNS cutover so visitors never hit a dead page.
