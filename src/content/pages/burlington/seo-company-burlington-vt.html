What to Ask Any SEO Company Before You Hire Them

If you're comparing a few SEO companies in Burlington, these four questions will tell you most of what you need to know:

  • Do I own the website and the content if we stop working together? Some agencies keep it, and you start from zero when you leave.
  • Is there a contract? Month to month means they have to keep earning it.
  • Will you write new pages, or only adjust the ones I have? Adjusting a five page site doesn't move rankings in a market this size.
  • What number do you report? Rankings and traffic are easy to make look good. Ask for leads from organic search.

Our answers are the same for everyone: you own it, month to month, new pages every month, and we report leads. There's a longer version of this list in 5 questions to ask any marketing agency.
