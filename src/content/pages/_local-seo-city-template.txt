LOCAL SEO CITY PAGE TEMPLATE
============================

How to add a new one (about 15 minutes per city):

1. Create src/content/pages/{city}/local-seo.html and paste everything below the
   line, then find and replace:
       {{CITY}}        -> Burlington
       {{CITY-SLUG}}   -> burlington
       {{STATE}}       -> Vermont
       {{STATE-ABBR}}  -> VT
2. Rewrite the copy. Do NOT ship it with only the city name swapped. Every page
   needs at least these three things unique to that city, or Google treats the
   set as doorway pages:
       - the neighborhoods, streets, or landmarks people there actually name
       - the specific nearby towns you compete against for those customers
       - something true about that market (size, seasonality, who dominates it)
3. Add a row to the "Local SEO" group in src/content/pages/service-offerings.html
   AND a matching entry to the ItemList schema in that page's head block.
4. Link to the new page from the city's web design page, and link back to it.
5. If the city has no web design page yet, delete the paragraph in the
   "What's Included" section that links to /{{CITY-SLUG}}/small-business-web-design/.
6. Check the form name. Every page needs its OWN Netlify form name, or all the
   city pages dump into one inbox and you cannot tell which page converted.
   The {{CITY}} replace handles it, but confirm both spots match:
       <form name="{{CITY}} Local SEO" ...>
       <input type="hidden" name="form-name" value="{{CITY}} Local SEO">
   Each name shows up as a separate form in the Netlify dashboard after deploy.
7. The in-page CTA buttons scroll to #lp-contact rather than leaving for
   /contact/. Keep it that way; the form is the whole point of the page.

Images used below are generic proof screenshots that are honest on any city page.
Swap them if you get city-specific proof.
