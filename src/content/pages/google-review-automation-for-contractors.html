How the Automation Works

When you finish a job, the customer gets a text and an email with a link straight to your Google review form. If they do not respond, the follow-ups go out on their own 2-3 times.

When a review lands, we reply to it for you. People trust a request that looks like it came from the company that was just at their house, and more of them click through. New reviews also post to your social accounts.
