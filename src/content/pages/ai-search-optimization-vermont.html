Where AI Answers Come From

ChatGPT does not keep a private list of businesses. When someone asks it for a roofer in Vermont, it runs a web search first, reads the pages that come back, and writes its answer from what it read.

So getting recommended means being in the material it reads: the pages that rank on Google, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, and anywhere else your name shows up online.

Those are all things you can change.

What the Work Involves

There is no button to press and no ad to buy. It comes down to five things.

  • A page for each service you want work in, aimed at the towns you serve, so there is something specific for a model to match a question to.
  • Answers written in plain language, high on the page, so a model can quote you without guessing what you meant.
  • Code behind the scenes that spells out your name, address, phone number, hours, and services in a format machines read.
  • A Google Business Profile that is complete, correct, and posted to, since AI answers lean on it heavily for local questions.
  • A steady flow of real reviews, because models repeat what other people say about you.
